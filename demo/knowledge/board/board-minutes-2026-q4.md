# Seaside Widgets Ltd: board minutes, Q4 2026 (fictional)

Present: the four directors. Not for circulation beyond the board.

1. **Project Lighthouse.** The board approved making an offer to acquire Harbourmoor Gears Ltd (fictional),
   a component supplier in Shoreham, for up to 4.2 million pounds. Due diligence to finish by
   the end of February 2027. Staff will be told only after contracts are signed.
2. **Restructuring.** The Worthing warehouse will close in June 2027; its 14 roles move to Hove.
   Consultation with affected staff starts in March 2027.
3. **Pay.** The salary budget for 2027 rises by 3.5 percent. Director bonuses are deferred until
   Lighthouse completes.
