# Seaside Widgets Ltd: expenses policy (fictional)

- Claims are submitted within 30 days with a receipt photo.
- Train travel: standard class; first class only for journeys over three hours.
- Hotel limit: 140 pounds per night in London, 100 pounds elsewhere.
- Client meals need a manager's approval in advance above 50 pounds per person.
- Expenses are paid with the next monthly salary run.
