# Seaside Widgets Ltd: holiday and leave policy (fictional)

- Every employee has 27 days of paid holiday a year, plus UK bank holidays.
- The office closes between Christmas Day and New Year's Day; three of the 27 days are used for this.
- Holiday requests go to your line manager at least two weeks in advance.
- Up to five unused days can be carried over to the next year, to be used by the end of March.
- Volunteering: two extra paid days a year for charity work.
